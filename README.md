# Unification List Builder

A private army list builder for **Warhammer: The Horus Heresy**, with a builder for each edition: **1st edition (Age of Darkness)**, **2nd edition** and **3rd edition**. Switch between them with the buttons at the top. It is packaged as an offline Android app and is also a web page. Pick a faction, Rite of War or detachments and units, set the wargear, and the builder costs the list, checks it against the force organisation and the rules, and prints it.

Get the app from **Releases → latest → `UnificationListBuilder.apk`**. Every push to `main` builds a new APK there.

Author: **Imperialist**.

## What's in the builder

### 1st Edition builder

#### Rules basis
- **Horus Heresy 1st edition is the master ruleset.** Its points and rules are never changed. Newer content is only added, behind opt-in tickboxes.
- **With every tickbox off, the list is pure 1.0.** This is checked with a comparison harness after every change.

#### Factions (362 units with the 1.0 rules)
- **All 18 Legiones Astartes**, plus Shattered Legions and Blackshields, each with their Legion rules, wargear, characters, Rites of War and Warlord Traits.
- **Solar Auxilia, Imperialis Militia, Questoris Knights, Mechanicum, Legio Custodes and Daemons of the Ruinstorm.**
- **Rites of War.** Their list-building effects are built in:
  - unit slot changes and compulsory Troops;
  - transports taken from other slots;
  - units joining from other lists, such as Brethren of Iron's automata and The Coils of the Hydra's Rewards of Treason (one dropdown entry);
  - the Company Command vehicle, the Primarch as an HQ, and rite-only wargear.
- **Force Organisation charts** and **missions (124)**, including the campaign and Exemplary Battles missions with their deployment maps.

#### Optional content (tickboxes)
- **Playtest rules:** updated profiles from the Errata and FAQ v1.1.
- **7th edition codex armies (203 units), converted to Heresy rules:**
  - The Awakened (Necrons), with the Forge World units from *Imperial Armour: The Fall of Orpheus* and the Necron Tomb Citadel;
  - Revenants of a Dead Empire (Craftworld Eldar, Harlequins and Dark Eldar), with the Forge World units from *Imperial Armour: The Doom of Mymeara*;
  - Remnants of Ullanor (Orks), including the optional Forge World Ramshackle table;
  - Xenos Beast Swarms (Tyranids) and Xenos Cults (Genestealer Cults);
  - Eldar Corsairs as their own army, with the Coterie Specialisations as Rites of War.

  These use the green Xenos colour scheme and a Xenos allegiance, Formations become Rites of War, and they take only their own Lords of War and Fortifications.
- **Post-Edition units (106 units):** 2nd and 3rd edition content converted to 1.0 rules, including:
  - Saturnine Terminators, Inductii (all Legions), Veteran Breacher and Assault Squads, Indomitus Terminators, Nullificators and Scouts;
  - Sicaran variants, Kratos, Macharius, Baneblade-pattern super-heavies and Marauders;
  - Sisters of Silence as their own army (Silent Judge, Knight Abyssal and the Cadres);
  - Solar Auxilia, Militia and Mechanicum additions, Armigers and the Acastus Asterius;
  - named characters from the 2nd edition books (Fafnir Rann, Argel Tal, Lucius, Horus Ascended, Fulgrim and Angron Transfigured and others);
  - Legiones Consularis: Stormseer, Esoterist, Pathfinder, Armistos, Mortificator, Overseer, Paladin of the Hekatonystika, Pack Thegn, Speaker of the Dead, Caster of Runes, Castellan and Dark Emissary;
  - 2nd edition Rites of War (Underworld Assault, Templar Assault, The Black Watch) and the Emperor's Children Legiones Hereticus rites.
- **Mournival Events content (fan expansion, 41 units):** the Mournival Events Rulebook 3.0 for 1.0:
  - Agents of the Emperor and Warmaster (Exodus, Vanus and Venenum Assassins, Rogue Psyker, Oblivion Knight-Centura, VIth Legion Watch Pack, Alpha Legion Sleeper Cell);
  - additional Legion, Solar Auxilia, Mechanicum, Militia and Custodes units;
  - Custom Characters for every faction, bought in 5-point Advances;
  - the Centurion Rites of War, Legion Outrider Band and Solar Auxilia Regiments;
  - Legion ammunition, archaeotech pistol rounds and exotic melee weapons as unit options;
  - a **game format** setting: **Centurion** (infantry-focused; the library hides unavailable units and the list checks the restricted-unit counts) or **Decurion** (a Hero, Leaders and 9-17 rank and file models on a warband chart, priced model by model);
  - reference sections for the Centurion and Decurion rules, psychic disciplines and the Zone Mortalis and Cityfight stratagems.

### 2nd Edition builder
- **Rules basis:** the 2nd edition rulebook, Liber Astartes, Liber Hereticus, Liber Imperium, Liber Mechanicum, the Legacies of the Age of Darkness PDFs (including Daemons of the Ruinstorm), The Battle for Beta-Garmon, Exemplary Battles Vol. 1 and The Siege of Cthonia are the master. No 1st edition content is mixed in.
- **Factions (443 units):**
  - all 18 Legions, Shattered Legions and Blackshields;
  - Solar Auxilia, Imperialis Militia, Mechanicum, Questoris Knights, Titan Legions, Legio Custodes, Sisters of Silence and Daemons of the Ruinstorm;
  - Clade Operatives of the Divisio Assassinorum and Cult Operatives of the Cults Abominatio.
- **List-building rules:**
  - Rites of War and Cohort Doctrines;
  - Solar Auxilia Tercios;
  - Militia Provenances;
  - Orders of High Techno-arcana;
  - Household Ranks and the Questoris Household chart;
  - Titan Maniple and Household Detachments;
  - Legiones Consularis and Legion armoury;
  - Ætheric Dominions;
  - optional 3rd Edition units (tickbox), converted to 2nd edition rules;
  - Oaths of Vengeance and Wrath, Legiones Decurion upgrades and Inductii templates;
  - the 2nd edition Lords of War and Primarch limits.
- **Missions (40 pages):** the rulebook missions and deployment maps, the 2023 Core Missions, Apex missions, Zone Mortalis and the Exemplary Battles.

### 3rd Edition builder
- **Rules basis:** Horus Heresy 3rd edition (Age of Darkness), with unit data compiled from Wahapedia and the Games Workshop downloads (FAQ, Legacies and Primary Detachments).
- **Army lists:** every 3rd edition army list: Legiones Astartes (all Legions, Shattered Legions and Blackshields), Solar Auxilia, Mechanicum, Imperialis Militia, Questoris Familia, Legio Titanicus, Talons of the Emperor and Ruinstorm Daemons, with full weapon profiles.
- **List-building rules:**
  - the Crusade Primary Detachment, with Prime slots and Prime Advantages (generic and faction-specific);
  - Apex and Auxiliary Detachments unlocked by filled High Command and Command slots, plus the Legion-specific Detachments and Battlefield Fortifications;
  - faction formations: Knight Households (with the Armiger, Automata, Yeomanry and Support Banner detachments), Solar Auxilia Tercios and Cohort Doctrines, Militia Provenances, the Mechanicum Apex and Auxiliary Detachments, and the Titan Ordinal;
  - Warlord (3,000+ points, needs a Paragon), Lord of War and Allied Detachments;
  - the shared 25% Warlord/Lord of War cap, the 50% Allied cap, Unique and 0-1 limits.
- **Print view and Settings**, as in the other editions.

### Using it
- **Live list checks:** slots, compulsory choices, unit limits, Rite of War restrictions, wargear restrictions and Warlord eligibility, each explained in plain language.
- **Unit cards:** the profile, wargear, weapon profiles, every special rule's text and a suggested base size. Dedicated Transports and attached units sit on their parent unit's card.
- **Rules reference:** special rules, unit types, weapons, wargear and psychic disciplines, all searchable.
- **Print view:** a clean printable list with the rules it uses. On Android it opens the print dialog, which includes Save as PDF.
- **My lists:** save, load and share lists as text.
- **Custom units** can be added to the library.
- **Phone layout:** a bottom tab bar (Army, Units, List, Rules) with points and list checks on the tabs.
- **Colour schemes:** Loyalist blue, Traitor red and Xenos green. The gear icon opens Settings: System, Light, Dark, **Black Book Light** (parchment pages, crimson headings) or **Black Book Dark** (black pages, gilt headings).
- **Data updates in the app:** Settings → Check for updates pulls the newest builder pages from this repository without reinstalling, and refreshes the screen.

### Companion books
- **Xenos: Alien Hosts of the Great Crusade** – a PDF codex supplement generated from the builder's 7th edition codex data (all six Xenos armies, including the Eldar Corsairs).
- **The Lost Armouries** – a PDF supplement of the Post-Edition units.

## Repository layout

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `app/src/main/assets/index.html` | **The 1st Edition builder.** One self-contained page holding all data and code (shipped encoded). Replace it to update the app. |
| `app/src/main/assets/2e.html` | **The 2nd Edition builder:** the rulebook, all four Liber books and the Legacies PDFs, with 2nd edition rules as the master. |
| `app/src/main/assets/3e.html` | **The 3rd Edition builder:** every army list from Wahapedia and the GW downloads, with the 3rd edition detachment system. |
| `app/src/main/java/com/unification/listbuilder/MainActivity.java` | The Android shell: a full-screen WebView with Android printing, clipboard and back-button support. |
| `app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml`, `app/src/main/res/` | App name, theme and launcher icon. |
| `app/build.gradle.kts`, `build.gradle.kts`, `settings.gradle.kts`, `gradle.properties` | Android build settings. |
| `app/signing/unification.keystore.b64` | The app's signing key, stored as text. Every build uses it so updates install over the old app. **Keep this repository private.** |
| `.github/workflows/build-apk.yml` | Builds the APK on every push to `main` and attaches it to the **latest** release. |
| `CHANGELOG.md` | What changed in each update. |

## Updating the app
The builder pages are shipped encoded (compressed and scrambled) so the data is not stored as plain text; they decode themselves when they load. Pushing a new `index.html`, `2e.html` or `3e.html` here is enough for phones to pick it up through Settings → Check for updates. A new APK is only needed when the Android code changes.

1. Replace `app/src/main/assets/index.html` with the new page. On GitHub, open the folder, choose **Add file → Upload files**, and drop the new `index.html` in.
2. Add a line to `CHANGELOG.md` saying what changed.
3. Commit to `main`. After a few minutes, the new APK appears under **Releases → latest**.
4. On your phone, download it and install it over the old one. Your saved lists are kept.

## Installing on Android
1. Open **Releases → latest** on your phone and tap `UnificationListBuilder.apk`.
2. Open the download. Android asks you to allow installs from your browser or Files app; allow it once.
3. Play Protect may say the app is from an unknown developer. Choose **Install anyway**.

Inside the app, lists are saved on the phone. To move a list to another device, copy it as text from **Save, load and share**.

## Building locally (optional)
Open the folder in Android Studio and choose *Build → Build APK(s)*. The signing key is unpacked automatically on the first build.
